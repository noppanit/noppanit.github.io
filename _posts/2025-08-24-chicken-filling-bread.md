## Filling Ingredients

- 1 medium onion
- 1 Japanese yam
- 1 chicken breast
- Salt, 1/2 tsp
- Sugar, 3 tsp
- Curry powder, 1/2 tsp
- Black pepper, 1/4 tsp

## Dough Ingredients

- Bread flour, 200 g
- All-purpose flour, 50 g
- Yeast, 10 g
- Milk, 125 g
- Butter, 25 g
- Salt, 2.5 g
- Sugar, 25 g
- Egg, 1/2

## Steps

1. Fry the yam first until yellow, and fry the onion separately.
2. Oven at 350°F for 15 minutes.
