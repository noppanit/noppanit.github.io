## Ingredients

- 250 g double cream
- 142 g 70% dark chocolate
- 4 g ground coffee (approx. 3/4 tbsp)
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 405 g full fat cream cheese
- 115 g caster sugar
- 10 g plain flour
- 5 g cocoa powder
- 1/2 tsp sea salt
- 145 g whole eggs, whisked (approx. 2 1/2-3 large)

## Steps

1. Bake at 230°C for 25 minutes.
2. Alternatively, 446°F and check at about 18-20 minutes since it's half of the ingredients.
