## Ingredients

- 1/2 Tbsp granulated sugar
- 1/2 tsp sea salt
- 1/2 lb COLD unsalted butter (2 sticks), diced into 1/4" pieces
- 6 to 7 Tbsp ice water

## Steps

Blind bake at 375°F for 25 minutes.
