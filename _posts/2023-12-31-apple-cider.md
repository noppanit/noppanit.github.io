## Ingredients

1. 10 apples; I used 2 of each variety
2. 1 cinnamon stick
3. 2-3 cloves
4. 1 orange; use only the flesh to avoid bitterness
5. 1/8 cup maple syrup; this can be adjusted later
6. 1/4 tsp of nutmeg
7. Water, just to cover the apples

## Steps

1. Combine all of the ingredients in an Instant Pot.
2. Cook for 20 minutes on low heat. After a natural release for 10 minutes, perform a quick release.
3. Mash the apples and oranges.
4. Transfer it to a pot and simmer for another 30 min.
5. Strain all the solids.
6. Simmer for another 30-45 minutes or until it reaches your desired thickness.
7. Adjust the sweetness to your liking.
8. Serve hot.
