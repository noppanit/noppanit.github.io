## Ingredients

1. Tuna
2. Lemon dressing: oil, lemon juice, egg yolk, vinegar, salt, pepper, sugar, mustard seed, apple, green onion
3. Cranberries
4. Tortilla
5. Lettuce
6. Cabbage

## Steps

1. Mix everything together to make a tuna salad.
2. Wrap it with the tortilla.
