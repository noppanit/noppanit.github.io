## Ingredients

1. AP flour, 250 g
2. Baking powder, 6 g
3. Baking soda, 2.5 g
4. Salt, 3 g
5. Oil, 10 g
6. Water, 165 g

## Steps

1. Mix dry ingredients.
2. Mix water with oil.
3. Rest for 20 minutes.
4. Shape into a rectangle and fold three times, resting 20 minutes between each fold.
