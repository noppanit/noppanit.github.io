## Dry / Base Ingredients (250g total mix)

1. AP flour — 125 g
2. Sugar — 67.5 g + 3 tbsp
3. Rice flour — 27.5 g
4. Corn starch — 22.5 g
5. Baking powder — 7.5 g
6. Salt — 1/4 tbsp
7. Toasted sesame seeds — 3 tbsp

## Wet & Add-Ins

1. Coconut milk — 200 g
2. Water — 180 g
3. Shredded coconut — 75 g

## Steps

1. You might need a bit more water, as the batter needs to be as thin as possible.
2. When frying, make sure the oil temp stays at 350°F.

## Half Batch — Dry / Base Ingredients (125g total mix)

1. AP flour — 62.5 g
2. Sugar — 33.75 g + 1 1/2 tbsp
3. Rice flour — 13.75 g
4. Corn starch — 11.25 g
5. Baking powder — 3.75 g
6. Salt — 3/8 tsp (half of 1/4 tbsp)
7. Toasted sesame seeds — 1 1/2 tbsp

## Half Batch — Wet & Add-Ins

1. Coconut milk — 100 g
2. Water — 90 g
3. Shredded coconut — 37.5 g
