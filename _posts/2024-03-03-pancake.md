## Ingredients for 2-3 people

1. 2 cups of flour
2. 3 tbsp. sugar
3. 4 tsps. baking power
4. 1 tsp. salt
5. 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
6. 2 eggs
7. 1/4 oil
8. 1 1/2 cup of milk
9. 1/2 tsp. vanilla

## Ingredients for 1-2 people

1. 1 cup flour
2. 1 1/2 tbsp. sugar
3. 2 tsp. baking power
4. 1/2 tsp. salt
5. 1/4 tsp. baking soda
6. 1 egg
7. 1/8 oil
8. 3/4 milk
9. 1/4 tsp. vanilla extract

## Steps

1. In one bowl, combine all of the dry ingredients.
2. In a measuring cup, combine all of the wet ingredients.
3. Pour the mixed wet ingredients into the dry bowl and mix well.
4. Let the mixture rest for 15 minutes.
